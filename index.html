Frequently asked questions

What is Messianic Shabbat Readings?

An app that provides weekly Torah portion (parashah) readings along with Haftarah and Brit Chadashah (New Testament) selections for Shabbat.

The readings aren't loading or the app shows an error.

Check your internet connection, then fully close and reopen the app. If it still fails, update to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play and try again. If the problem continues, email me with a description of what you see.

The reading shown doesn't match this week's portion.

Check that your device's date, time, and time zone are set automatically. Some communities also follow different calendars (for example, Israel and the Diaspora can differ on certain weeks). Email me the date and portion you expected and I'll look into it.

How do I restore my purchases?

If the app offers paid features, open the app's settings and choose "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy them.

How do I update the app?

Open the App Store (iPhone/iPad) or Google Play (Android), search for the app, and tap Update if one is available.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email me at the address above. Your feedback is welcome and helps shape future updates.
